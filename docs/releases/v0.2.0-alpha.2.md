# ITP v0.2.0-alpha.2

## Release overview

Alpha 2 establishes ITP as a deterministic, vendor-neutral operational
intelligence platform. Collector evidence now flows through canonical assets,
sites, infrastructure state, Operations, and Service Health before reaching
the Operations Wallboard and managed dashboards.

## Major capabilities

- Canonical infrastructure, inventory, site, estate, and virtualisation models
- Multi-source asset fusion and duplicate suppression
- Multi-customer deployment profiles and multi-site estate rollups
- Deterministic issues, risks, recommendations, and service evaluation
- Capability-aware Grafana dashboard provisioning
- Profile-isolated, deterministic release-evidence generation

## Supported infrastructure domains

Alpha 2 models networking, switching, wireless, WAN, firewalls, security,
compute, storage, printing, inventory, lifecycle, collector health, and
virtualisation. Domain availability follows enabled collector capabilities;
missing evidence remains `Unknown` or `Not Enabled`.

## Virtualisation

Read-only adapters support VMware vSphere, Microsoft Hyper-V, and Proxmox VE.
They normalise managers, clusters, hosts, virtual machines, containers, storage,
networks, and snapshots while retaining provider evidence.

Service-impact propagation is conservative. Management-plane loss or storage
capacity pressure does not automatically prove a workload outage.

## Operations and Service Health

The Operations Engine promotes canonical evidence into explainable Active
Issues, Operational Risks, and Recommendations with deterministic priority.
Service Health evaluates vendor-neutral services by site and across the estate,
recording status, summary, affected assets, last change, and evidence.

## Operations Wallboard

The Alpha 2 Wallboard provides a generated, current-state operational view with
overall and service health, active issue counts, data freshness, infrastructure
status, collector state, and one consolidated Action Required queue.

Capability-gated panels reflow deterministically. Virtualisation uses concise
service cards, evidence-based impact, human-readable action labels, and
profile-isolated example-corporate, VMware, Hyper-V, and Proxmox evidence scenarios.

## Validation

- The complete deterministic fixture-based test suite passes offline.
- Evidence generation is deterministic for all four release scenarios.
- example-school and example-corporate deployment profiles validate independently.
- Generated Wallboards use classic Grafana JSON with stable UIDs.
- Tests require no live collectors, cloud services, or production credentials.

## Known Alpha limitations

- Current canonical Infrastructure, Operations, and Service Health state is
  file-backed and does not retain durable history.
- Collector, provider, and service-domain coverage remains incomplete.
- Optional or untrusted evidence produces `Unknown`.
- Platform high availability is not included.
- Configuration, credentials, and upgrade review remain operator-managed.

## Next milestone

**OPS-008 — Scheduler and State History** will introduce unified analysis
scheduling, durable timestamped state, deterministic retention and replay, and
change-over-time evidence. Those capabilities are roadmap items and are not
implemented in Alpha 2.
